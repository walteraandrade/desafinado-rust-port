# 02 ai (crates/ai)

## Changes made
- Deleted 24 modules with no reference anywhere in the repo (grepped crates, docs, README, scripts), about 3000 lines with their tests:
  - editor/: code_block_lines, frontmatter_marks, interaction_state, keep_in_view, minimal_change, motion_field, refine_draft, refine_input, review_controls, selection_marks, tooltip_space, word_reveal.
  - ui/: app_menus, file_commands, history_keys, idle, key_status, keys, library_fold, library_layout, menu_nav, page_mode, platform, preferences_shortcut.
  - dev_log.rs.
  - editor/mod.rs, ui/mod.rs and lib.rs now list only what remains.
- Removed unused pub items: `CallResult` (errors.rs), `DECISION_PROVIDER` (review_mode.rs), `MIN_RETRY_MS` (request.rs), and the unused motion constants in ui/list_motion.rs (MOVE_MS, ENTER_MS, LEAVE_MS, EASE_OUT_*, ENTER_OFFSET_PX).

## Proposed, not made

- 21 tests fail `cargo clippy --all-targets -D warnings` with `assert_is_empty` (e.g. `assert!(x.is_empty())` in array_stream, chat_stream, edits, place, notes, review_stream, list_motion, jobs, inline_diff, shown_cards, generation). Pre-existing, from the installed clippy version, not from my change. Fixing means rewriting asserts across files I own; left alone because the lib is clean and the fix is toolchain-dependent (the suggested `assert_eq!(x, [])` is worse). Decide whether to `#[expect]` it workspace-wide in root Cargo.toml (layer owning it).
- Many pub items are only used inside their own file (e.g. in notes.rs, schemas.rs, model_choice.rs ModelStorage/read/write_model_choice, request.rs RequestKind limits, generation.rs FetchGenerationOptions). Candidates for `pub(crate)` or deletion; not verified one by one.
- Cargo.toml `regex = "1"` is not `.workspace = true`; cross-layer (root Cargo.toml).
